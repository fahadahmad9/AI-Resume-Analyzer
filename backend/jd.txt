Job Description - Entry-Level Software Engineer

We are looking for an entry-level Software Engineer with a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.

Required qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field.
- Knowledge of Python, JavaScript, or another programming language.
- Understanding of object-oriented programming and data structures.
- Basic knowledge of SQL and relational databases.
- Familiarity with Git and version control.
- Experience or academic exposure to REST APIs.
- Strong problem-solving and communication skills.

Preferred qualifications
- Experience with React or Node.js.
- Familiarity with PostgreSQL or MongoDB.
- Exposure to Docker.
- Basic knowledge of cloud platforms such as Google Cloud or AWS.
- Familiarity with FastAPI or Django.
